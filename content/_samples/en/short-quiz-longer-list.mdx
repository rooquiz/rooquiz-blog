The instinct is that a longer quiz qualifies harder, so the leads are better. The arithmetic says otherwise, and it is not close.

## The arithmetic

A twelve-question quiz at 34% completion and a five-question quiz at 71% completion, from the same traffic, do not produce comparable lists. The short quiz produces more than twice the leads, and the segmentation you lost lives in the seven questions you cut — questions that, on inspection, mostly correlated with the five you kept.

## What to do with the questions you cut

Do not throw them away. Move them into the post-result sequence, where the person has already said yes once and answering costs them nothing. You get the same data, later, from people who are actually worth segmenting.

> The quiz is for getting permission. The email sequence is for getting detail.
