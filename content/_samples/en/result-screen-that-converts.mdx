Every quiz funnel has one screen that does the actual selling, and it is almost never the one people optimise. It is the result screen — the moment a person finds out what they are, and decides in the same breath whether you are worth an email address.

## What the four seconds are spent on

Watch a session recording and you will see the same sequence: read the result label, scan for whether it feels true, look for what happens next. That is it. Anything on the screen that does not serve one of those three jobs is costing you attention you cannot buy back.

- **The label** should be a noun they would use about themselves, not a grade.
- **The evidence** should quote their own answers back at them.
- **The next step** should be one thing, phrased as a continuation rather than a form.

## The mistake almost everyone makes

The default result screen explains the _scoring_. Nobody cares how the sausage is made. They care whether the sausage is about them.

Replace "You scored 14 of 20 on audience clarity" with "You know who you are for — you just have not written it down anywhere they can read it." Same data, and the second one earns the email.
