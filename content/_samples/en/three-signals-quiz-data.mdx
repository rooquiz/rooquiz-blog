Completion rate is a scoreboard, not a diagnosis. It tells you something is wrong without telling you where. These three numbers tell you where.

## Drop-off position

Bucket abandonments by question index. A clean funnel loses people evenly; a broken one loses them at a specific question. That question is either too personal, too vague, or too early.

## Answer skew

If 80% of respondents pick the same option, the question is not discriminating — it is decoration. Either rewrite the options so they split the audience, or cut the question and get the completion rate back.

## Revision rate

How often people change an answer before submitting. High revision on a question means the wording is ambiguous. This is the cheapest signal to collect and the one nearly nobody looks at.
